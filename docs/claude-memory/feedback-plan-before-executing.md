The user works in plan mode first and approves before execution. They rejected a question batch that asked too much at once and prefer short, specific questions. They want honest flags about risks (public repos, personal accounts, credentials), not reassurance.

**Why:** the work touches company repos, credentials and spend.
**How to apply:** present a plan and wait for approval before building; ask only questions that change what you would do; flag exposure and cost risks early, in plain words.
